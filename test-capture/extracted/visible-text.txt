[h2] What are common questions about image compression?
[dt] Do my images leave my device during compression?
[dd] No. Image compression happens on your device using browser-based processing; selected image files are not sent to our server for compression. The page still needs an internet connection to load, but the image contents remain in your browser during the compression operation.
[dt] What does image compression cost?
[dd] Nothing. The tool is free to use and does not require an account or subscription. Select images, adjust the quality setting, and download the compressed copies without paying. Your internet connection may be needed to load the page, but compression itself runs in your browser rather than sending the image files to a paid service.
[dt] Which image formats can I compress?
[dd] JPG, PNG, and WebP are the primary supported formats for compression. The tool also accepts most common image formats, but output behavior can depend on the source type and browser support. For reliable results, use JPG, PNG, or WebP images and check the downloaded file before sharing it.
[dt] Will compressing an image reduce its quality?
[dd] Yes. Lossy compression can reduce image detail, especially at lower quality settings. Use the quality control to balance smaller files against visual clarity, then preview the downloaded result. PNG may remain lossless depending on the source and output encoding, so compare the result rather than assuming every format behaves identically.
