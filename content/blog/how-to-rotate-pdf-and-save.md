To rotate a PDF and save the change, select the sideways pages in a PDF rotation tool, choose an angle, and export the updated document. A viewer’s temporary rotate control may change only how a page looks on screen. With the browser-based [Rotate PDF tool](/tools/rotate-pdf), download the rotated PDF to keep the orientation in the saved file.

## Why do PDF pages sometimes open sideways?

Scanned documents are a common cause: pages may have been fed into a scanner in landscape orientation or upside down. A PDF can also contain pages with different orientations, especially if pages were combined from multiple sources. Rotating a page in a viewer can be temporary; the original file may retain its previous page orientation until you create and save an updated PDF.

## How do you rotate one page or all pages in a PDF?

1. Open the [Rotate PDF tool](/tools/rotate-pdf) and choose a PDF from your device.
2. Review the page thumbnails. Select individual pages that need adjustment, or use **Select all** for the whole document.
3. Choose a clockwise rotation of 90, 180, or 270 degrees.
4. Click **Rotate PDF**, check the output, and choose **Download PDF**.

The tool previews pages and applies rotation locally in your browser. If a document has a mixture of correct and sideways pages, select only the pages that need correction so the others keep their current orientation. You can repeat the process with a different selection if needed.

## How do you make a PDF rotation permanent?

Download the rotated result as a new PDF. The change is saved in that downloaded file; merely turning a page in a viewer or leaving the tool open does not replace your original. Open the downloaded PDF in a separate viewer and check each changed page before you share it or delete the original.

The [browser-based rotation tool](/tools/rotate-pdf) processes the PDF on your device, but downloaded files still need to be handled according to your own retention and sharing requirements. Keep the source until you have confirmed the new copy is correct.

## What should you check after rotating PDF pages?

Confirm that the intended pages—not just the first page—are upright, and check that pages you did not select remain unchanged. Rotation changes the page orientation without intentionally rasterizing the content, but review the downloaded copy in a viewer you trust. Password-protected or corrupted PDFs may not process; retain the source file as a fallback.

## What are common questions about rotating and saving PDFs?

### How do I rotate just one page in a PDF?

Open the [Rotate PDF tool](/tools/rotate-pdf), load your file, and select only the page thumbnail that needs correction. Choose a rotation angle, run the operation, and download the result. Check the saved PDF afterward to confirm the other pages kept their original orientation and the selected page now reads correctly.

### How can I rotate every page at once?

After loading the document, choose **Select all** so each page is selected, then choose the desired clockwise angle and rotate. Review the result before downloading. If some pages were already correctly oriented, select only the pages that need a change instead of rotating the entire document.

### Why does the rotated PDF look unchanged after I close the tool?

The updated PDF is a separate output; the tool does not overwrite your original file automatically. Download the rotated copy and open that file to see the saved orientation. A viewer’s own rotate button may only adjust the current display and may not save any change to the document.

### Is my PDF uploaded when I rotate its pages?

No. The [Rotate PDF tool](/tools/rotate-pdf) creates page previews and applies rotation in your browser; the selected PDF is not sent to Convaudit’s server for processing. Your browser still needs the site to load, and its available memory can affect how large a document it can handle.
