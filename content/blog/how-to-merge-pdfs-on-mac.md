To **merge PDF files on Mac**, first decide the order you want in the finished document. macOS Preview can combine PDFs using its page thumbnails, while a browser-based merger is another option when you want to add files, rearrange them, and download one combined PDF. In either case, keep copies of the originals until you have checked the result.

## Combine PDFs with Preview

Open a PDF in Preview and show the page thumbnail sidebar. Drag another PDF into the sidebar at the position where its pages should appear. You can move thumbnails to adjust order, then save or export a separate combined copy. Menu labels may vary by macOS version, so check the saved file before replacing any original.

## Common questions about merging PDFs on Mac

### Can Preview merge PDFs without installing another app?

Preview is included with macOS and can combine PDF pages through its thumbnail sidebar. Open one document, reveal thumbnails, then drag pages from another PDF into the desired position. Since menus and behavior can vary between versions, save a new copy and reopen it to confirm that every page appears in the intended order.

### How do I keep pages in the right order?

Before combining files, write down the desired document sequence or rename copies with numbered prefixes. After adding them, inspect the thumbnails and move pages before saving. Finally, open the merged PDF and check its page count and section transitions; filenames alone do not guarantee that the pages were combined in the correct order.

### Will merging PDFs reduce their quality?

Combining pages normally does not require changing their visual content, but the result can depend on the software and any options used during export. Avoid choosing a setting that compresses or rasterizes pages unless that is your goal. Compare a few pages in the saved document with the originals, including text and images.

The page itself still needs an internet connection to load. For sensitive documents, review the tool's data-handling details and retain your original files.
