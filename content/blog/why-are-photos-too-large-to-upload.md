## What makes a photo file large?

Pixel dimensions matter: a photo several thousand pixels wide contains more image data than a small profile picture. Format also affects size. JPEG is commonly used for photographs, while PNG can preserve transparency and sharp edges but may produce a larger file. Compression settings affect the balance between file size and visible detail.

## Five ways to reduce a photo before upload

1. **Check the required limit.** Note the maximum file size and any required pixel dimensions before editing.
2. **Resize oversized images.** If the destination displays a small image, exporting far more pixels than it needs can add weight without improving what viewers see.
3. **Choose a suitable format.** Use a format accepted by the website; JPEG often works well for ordinary photos, while PNG is useful when transparency matters.
4. **Adjust compression gradually.** Lower quality in small steps and inspect fine details, text, and faces after each export.

## Common questions about oversized photo uploads

### Why does a photo exceed an upload limit?

High pixel dimensions, detailed scenes, and a format with less efficient compression can all increase file size. Phone photos may also contain more pixels than a website needs to display. Check both the site's maximum file size and its preferred dimensions; reducing the wrong property may not solve the rejection.

### Will compressing a photo make it blurry?

It can, especially when using strong lossy compression or resizing too aggressively. Make a copy, adjust quality gradually, and inspect faces, text, and fine edges at the size people will actually view. If the result looks soft, restore some quality or try resizing to the required dimensions instead.

### Should I use JPEG or PNG for an upload?

Follow the destination site's accepted formats first. JPEG is often a practical choice for photographs because it can produce smaller files, while PNG is useful for graphics that need transparency or crisp edges. Converting formats can change appearance, so compare the exported image and keep your original.

### Can every image be reduced to the site's exact size limit?

Not reliably without affecting appearance. The amount of reduction depends on image content, dimensions, format, and quality settings. If a portal requires a strict maximum, aim a little below the limit and verify the downloaded file's size. Do not rely on a percentage estimate without checking the actual result.
